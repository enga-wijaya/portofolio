    POSITION

    DURATION

    03. FPSO Topside Module

    POSITION

    Senior Project Administrator & Document Control

    DURATION

    [7th Mar 2023] – [30th July 2024]

Historically fabricated in Asia, recent milestones have seen the first major modules manufactured locally in Ghana at the Port of Takoradi - West Africa.
