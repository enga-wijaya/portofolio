My Timeline of Project

I have accumulated 20 years of experience in the oil and gas industry, working across various key roles. Starting as a Project Secretary, I managed critical administrative functions, including scheduling, internal communications, and document management. Over time, I transitioned into Project Management, where I was responsible for overseeing project timelines, budgets, and deliverables, ensuring that projects were executed efficiently and aligned with industry standards. My role allowed me to develop a deep understanding of the operational intricacies and challenges within the sector.

In addition to project management, I gained significant expertise in Document Control, ensuring the proper handling and organization of essential project documentation to maintain compliance and facilitate smooth project execution. Furthermore, as a Talent Acquisition Specialist, I was involved in identifying and securing high-caliber professionals to meet the unique demands of oil and gas projects. My combined experience in administration, project leadership, and talent management has enabled me to add value across multiple dimensions of any project I am involved in.
